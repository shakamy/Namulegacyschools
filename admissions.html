Admissions

We welcome applications from Creche through Senior Secondary. Here's how to get your child enrolled.

Process

How admissions works

  1. Submit an application

    Complete the online form below with your child's details, or visit the school office to pick up a paper form.

  2. Assessment & interview

    Depending on the class being applied for, your child may sit a brief entrance assessment, and parents meet with our admissions team.

  3. Offer & documentation

    Successful applicants receive an admission offer. We'll ask for birth certificate, previous school report (if applicable), and passport photos.

  4. Registration & resumption

    Complete registration payment and receive your resumption date, class placement, and uniform/supplies list.

Levels Offered

  • 1Creche & Pre-Nursery
  • 2Nursery 1 – 2
  • 3Primary 1 – 6
  • 4Junior Secondary

Documents Needed

• Birth certificate / age declaration
• Two passport photographs
• Previous school report (if transferring)
• Immunization record
