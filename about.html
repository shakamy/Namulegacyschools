Our Story

About Namu Legacy Schools

A school built on the belief that diligent work and sincere worship shape lasting character.

History

How we started

Namu Legacy Schools Ltd was incorporated on 30 January 2019 (RC 1557442), founded on the conviction that children flourish when academic rigor is paired with strong character formation.

Since then, the school has grown into a trusted institution at No. 1, Yawori Street, Garki, Abuja, FCT — serving families who want their children known personally and pushed to their full potential.

Mission & Vision

What we're working toward

Our Mission

To provide a balanced, values-driven education that equips every child academically, morally, and spiritually for a life of purpose.

Our Vision

To be a leading school in Abuja recognized for producing disciplined, capable, and God-conscious young people.

Our Motto

Work and Worship

Two words that shape daily life at Namu Legacy — diligence in the classroom, and devotion beyond it.

W

Work

We teach children to take ownership of their learning — showing up prepared, trying hard problems, and taking pride in a job done well.

W

Worship
